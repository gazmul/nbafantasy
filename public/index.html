Controls & match rules
WASD / arrows Move · Space Hold and release to shoot · E Pass / switch · Shift Boost
On mobile, use the left thumbstick and right action buttons. Portrait court: cobalt attacks up, ivory down. The direction beside your player name follows your screen.
Release inside the light zone for the best shot. Shoot becomes Steal / Block on defence. Tap Shoot close to your attacking hoop for a dunk. Score three consecutive baskets with one player to ignite them for 25 seconds. Fire adds shot, speed and stamina bonuses. Selected weekly positional leaders start with a 30-second fire bonus. Demo picks are labelled; weekly winners require owner confirmation. Two-minute matches, a 24-second possession clock and overtime for ties.
How weekly stats shape your players

Weekly totals ÷ games played create per-game abilities: points → finishing; threes made → range; assists → passing; rebounds → rebounding; steals + blocks → defence. Base abilities run from 45 to 90, reaching 90 at 35 points, 5 threes, 12 assists, 15 rebounds or 4 steals + blocks per game.

Score advantage = score difference ÷ the higher absolute score × 6, capped at ±6 ability points. Height affects reach, size and pace; weight affects pace. Both sides begin 0–0. Shot timing, distance and defensive pressure affect every attempt. These are test tuning rules, not official ratings.
